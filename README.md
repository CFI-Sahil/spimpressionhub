## 🌟 About the Project

**SP Impression Hub** is a purpose-built website for a local creative printing and citizen-service business.

The platform combines two major business divisions:

### 🎨 Custom Printing & Personalized Gifts

Showcasing products and services such as:

- 🎁 Personalized Photo Gifts
- 👕 Custom T-Shirt Printing
- ☕ Photo Ceramic & Magic Mugs
- 🔑 Acrylic Keychains
- 🖼️ Photo Frames & Collages
- 🪪 PVC ID Cards & Lanyards
- 🏷️ Custom Stickers & Labels
- 💳 Visiting Cards & Letterheads
- 📄 Digital & Promotional Printing
- 💍 Wedding & Event Printing
- 🎉 Birthday & Occasion Gifts
- 📦 Corporate & Promotional Merchandise

### 💻 Kripa Online / CSC Services

Providing information about various citizen and online services:

- 🪪 PAN Card
- 👤 E-Shram Card
- 🌾 7/12 & 8A Extract
- 🏠 Ration Card
- 📜 Life Certificate
- 👮 Police Verification
- 🌱 Crop Insurance
- 🏛️ Government Schemes
- 🎓 Educational & Scholarship Forms
- 🗳️ Voter ID
- ❤️ Ayushman Card
- 🏥 ABHA Card
- 🏢 Business & License Services
- 🔐 KYC Services
- 📋 Various Government & Citizen Services

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

The website follows a **clean, professional, modern local-business aesthetic**.

### Design Principles

- ✨ Professional without being overly corporate
- 🧹 Clean and minimal visual hierarchy
- 🎯 Clear service discovery
- 🤝 Approachable and trustworthy
- 📐 Structured spacing and typography
- 📱 Responsive across devices
- 🎞️ Subtle, purposeful motion

### 🎨 Visual Language

The interface uses a carefully controlled visual system built around:

- 🔵 Deep Navy
- 🟠 Orange
- 🤍 Warm White / Cream
- ⚫ Charcoal Typography
- ▫️ Subtle Borders
- 🌫️ Controlled Shadows

The design intentionally avoids excessive visual effects and focuses on **clarity, usability, and brand consistency**.
